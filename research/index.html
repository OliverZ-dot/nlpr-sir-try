Research

Six problems, one person in view.

From the iris camera to the privacy of the template, the group builds the pieces of recognition that still work when cooperation is thin.

01

Iris Recognition

The full iris pipeline, from segmentation and encoding to recognition when the eye is moving, distant, or only partly open.

02

Ocular Biometrics

Sclera, periocular skin, and fused ocular regions for identity when a full iris texture is not available.

03

Light-Field Photography

Microlens imaging for refocus, depth, liveness, and iris capture across a much larger volume.

04

Biometric Imaging Systems

Optics, sensors, and capture software for iris on the move, including a multi-person system at 10 meters.

05

Trustworthy Recognition

Privacy, federated learning, adversarial defense, and demographic coverage for biometric systems that have to be believed.

06

Human Perception

Bodies, hands, gait-scale motion, and millimeter-wave sensing when the face is not the only signal.
