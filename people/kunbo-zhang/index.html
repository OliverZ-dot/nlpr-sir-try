Kunbo Zhang

  • IJCB 2020 Best Paper Runner-up

Kunbo Zhang is an associate professor at NLPR, MAIS, CASIA. He received the B.E. degree in automation from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2006, and the M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in mechanical engineering from Stony Brook University in 2008 and 2011. From 2011 to 2016 he was a machine-vision engineer in the Advanced Manufacturing Engineering group at Nexteer Automotive in Michigan. His research focuses on light-field photography, biometric imaging, robot vision, human-robot interaction, and intelligent manufacturing. The all-in-focus iris camera developed with the group received the Best Paper Award runner-up at IJCB 2020.
