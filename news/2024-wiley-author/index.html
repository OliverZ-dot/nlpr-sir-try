Light-field survey selected for the Wiley China Excellent Author Program

The IET Computer Vision survey on light-field imaging was included in the Wiley China Excellent Author Program for October–December 2024.

A comprehensive research on light field imaging: theory and application, by Fei Liu, Yunlong Wang, Qing Yang, Shubo Zhou, and Kunbo Zhang (IET Computer Vision), was selected for the Wiley China Excellent Author Program, October–December 2024.
